## How to read the map

Read the physical loop from system to signal, record, estimated state, future/decision, action and back to the system. The observer is part of that physical world. The lower evidence loop describes how researchers revise the account; it is not another physical law or an autonomous discovery service. The three middle gates separately ask whether a distinction is observable, predictive and useful for control.

The solid arrows show declared mathematical/model relationships, not blanket empirical certification. The outlined observer description is a modelling boundary. Physical self-maintenance is a required biological extension; subjective experience remains open. Nothing here identifies a sensory record, a Bayesian belief or a diagram with conscious experience.

## Present implementation and publication boundary

A local finite enzyme-model prototype now exercises noisy observation, joint uncertainty over state/mechanism/calibration, future prediction, paid measurement and terminal action. Tests distinguish sensor ambiguity from missing predictive state and from limited control opportunity. A form-specific observation can improve identification and prediction while adding little decision value. Exact conditional information ceilings now check whether a proposed improvement target is attainable before further policy search.

Those are scoped local model results. This page releases the conceptual relationships and the elementary decision-ceiling derivation, not the private experiment code, unpublished mechanisms, clinical evidence or a public reproducibility package. The numerical research is not presented here as independently published validation. Manuscript citations below identify the author’s programme sources and their stated premises; they are not independent replications.

## E01 · World → accessible signal

**Status:** Declared observation law.

A measurement is represented by a conditional law L(y | x, a, calibration). A noisy or many-to-one observation need not identify x. The current prototype uses a nondestructive likelihood; an invasive instrument needs a joint state-transition/outcome law.

**Required conditions:** Specify measured quantity, instrument, preparation, timing, calibration, interference and whether observation disturbs the system.

**Failure or limit:** A calibration or observation model fails on withheld measurements; distinct target-relevant states remain observationally identical.

**Sources:** [The Observer and the World: Predictive state, lawful forgetting, and the geometry of empirical reality](https://boundedness-atlas.madmanmuzza.chatgpt.site/papers/current-07/) — Retained manuscript abstract, PDF page 1; closing PDF pages 21, 22; [The Temporal Architecture of Living Nature: Predictive state, robust viability, and the recursive construction of biological futures](https://boundedness-atlas.madmanmuzza.chatgpt.site/papers/current-05/) — Retained manuscript abstract, PDF page 1; closing PDF pages 22.

<a id="e02"></a>
## E02 · Signal → retained record

**Status:** Conditional mathematical relation.

A record r=q(y) is an encoding. A finite bijection has a two-sided inverse; a many-to-one map does not preserve every distinction. A finite code cannot injectively represent every real value in an interval. Any lossless or sufficiently accurate analogue bridge requires a stated signal class and error contract.

**Required conditions:** Preserve units, source identity, timestamp and encoding. Distinguish mathematical reconstruction error from floating-point and sensor error.

**Failure or limit:** A permitted later question distinguishes inputs collapsed to the same retained record.

**Sources:** [The Observer and the World: Predictive state, lawful forgetting, and the geometry of empirical reality](https://boundedness-atlas.madmanmuzza.chatgpt.site/papers/current-07/) — Retained manuscript abstract, PDF page 1; closing PDF pages 21, 22; [Epistemic Type Safety for Generative AI: Witnessed Assertion, Fail-Closed Kernels, and Why the Model Need Not Be the World](https://boundedness-atlas.madmanmuzza.chatgpt.site/papers/current-01/) — Retained manuscript abstract, PDF page 1; closing PDF pages 15.

<a id="e03"></a>
## E03 · Ordered record → estimated state

**Status:** Conditional probability and predictive-state requirement.

Bayes updating multiplies a declared prior by the observation likelihood and normalizes positive evidence. A useful retained representation must preserve the future laws it claims to support. An impossible observation is a model-adequacy failure, not permission to invent certainty.

**Required conditions:** Keep mechanism, detector and physical-state uncertainty correlated; specify the hypothesis family. Finite-catalogue updating does not discover arbitrary new mechanisms.

**Failure or limit:** Identical retained states support different conditional future laws under an admitted common challenge.

**Sources:** [Predictive Closure: State, action, and the experimental compression of history](https://boundedness-atlas.madmanmuzza.chatgpt.site/papers/current-10/) — Retained manuscript abstract, PDF page 1; closing PDF pages 31; [The Observer and the World: Predictive state, lawful forgetting, and the geometry of empirical reality](https://boundedness-atlas.madmanmuzza.chatgpt.site/papers/current-07/) — Retained manuscript abstract, PDF page 1; closing PDF pages 21, 22.

<a id="e04"></a>
## E04 · Estimated state → future law

**Status:** State-law descent and conditional prediction.

A future law descends to a representation exactly when it is constant on that representation's history fibres. Given an adequate model, the forecast is the mixture of future kernels over the current belief. Richer future tests may require a finer state.

**Required conditions:** Declare future tests, continuation domains, horizon, outcome, tolerance and mechanisms. An associative matrix product requires the retained intermediate state to support its factorization.

**Failure or limit:** Held-out common futures distinguish histories assigned the same proposed state; a numerical solver disagrees beyond its error tolerance.

**Sources:** [The Observer and the World: Predictive state, lawful forgetting, and the geometry of empirical reality](https://boundedness-atlas.madmanmuzza.chatgpt.site/papers/current-07/) — Retained manuscript abstract, PDF page 1; closing PDF pages 21, 22; [Predictive Closure: State, action, and the experimental compression of history](https://boundedness-atlas.madmanmuzza.chatgpt.site/papers/current-10/) — Retained manuscript abstract, PDF page 1; closing PDF pages 31.

<a id="e05"></a>
## E05 · Future law → feasible decision

**Status:** Conditional decision rule.

For a declared objective and allowed action set, choose an action by its conditional expected utility or required success threshold. A code cell supports one acceptable deterministic action only when its states' acceptable-action sets have a nonempty common intersection.

**Required conditions:** Specify the objective, risk rule, domain, action availability, costs and delay. Static action sufficiency does not establish recursive controller sufficiency.

**Failure or limit:** A code merges states requiring incompatible actions; additional prediction accuracy does not improve the declared decision enough to justify its cost.

**Sources:** [From Predictive State to Viable Action: Action Sufficiency, Safe Diagnosis, and the Operational Recoverability Bound](https://boundedness-atlas.madmanmuzza.chatgpt.site/papers/current-11/) — Retained manuscript abstract, PDF page 1; closing PDF pages 10, 11.

## E06 · Action → changed world

**Status:** Mechanistic model; empirical bridge required.

An intervention selects a physical transition law and thereby changes subsequent conditions. Environmental and organism variables can both be affected. In the local toy model, only specified terminal rate clamps are implemented.

**Required conditions:** A causal effect requires an intervention design or justified mechanism, not correlation alone. Real actuation strength, invasiveness, conservation and energetic costs need calibration.

**Failure or limit:** The purported intervention cannot be implemented or does not produce its stated transition/outcome law.

**Sources:** [The Temporal Architecture of Living Nature: Predictive state, robust viability, and the recursive construction of biological futures](https://boundedness-atlas.madmanmuzza.chatgpt.site/papers/current-05/) — Retained manuscript abstract, PDF page 1; closing PDF pages 22; [From Predictive State to Viable Action: Action Sufficiency, Safe Diagnosis, and the Operational Recoverability Bound](https://boundedness-atlas.madmanmuzza.chatgpt.site/papers/current-11/) — Retained manuscript abstract, PDF page 1; closing PDF pages 10, 11.

<a id="e07"></a>
## E07 · Decision → next measurement

**Status:** Costed diagnosis; model-based policy.

A diagnostic is useful only through the distinctions and decisions it can change. For two equally likely hypotheses under a fixed diagnostic, minimum classification error is (1-TV(P1,P2))/2. This is an identification/selection result, not automatically a biological failure probability.

**Required conditions:** Charge calibration, measurement, delay and harm. Compare with strong fixed schedules at the same budget. The present local policy is myopic, not globally optimal.

**Failure or limit:** The diagnostic cannot distinguish relevant alternatives, arrives too late, or its decision gain disappears against a matched comparator.

**Sources:** [From Predictive State to Viable Action: Action Sufficiency, Safe Diagnosis, and the Operational Recoverability Bound](https://boundedness-atlas.madmanmuzza.chatgpt.site/papers/current-11/) — Retained manuscript abstract, PDF page 1; closing PDF pages 10, 11; [The Observer and the World: Predictive state, lawful forgetting, and the geometry of empirical reality](https://boundedness-atlas.madmanmuzza.chatgpt.site/papers/current-07/) — Retained manuscript abstract, PDF page 1; closing PDF pages 21, 22.

## E08 · Bounds and lawful composition constrain every connection

**Status:** Declared domain plus conditional mathematics.

Conserved quantities, capacities and action/resource budgets restrict admissible states and transitions. Compatible function composition is associative, preserving order. Boundedness alone does not imply an associative binary operation, a unique coordinate, Markov closure or universal physics. A specific additive generator requires the relevant composition and regularity premises.

**Required conditions:** Retain each operation's domain, clock, units, preparation, calibration and composition assumptions. Check physical conservation separately from numerical normalization.

**Failure or limit:** A proposed connection violates its type, invariant, budget or continuation domain; a coordinate is used without the mechanism or premises that select it.

## E09 · Observer's physical self-maintenance

**Status:** Programme extension; not implemented as biology in the current loop.

The observer is itself physical. A biological extension must include its material condition, sensor/effector limits, memory costs and possible viable continuation. The present inference program does not establish an organism's metabolism or self-maintenance.

**Required conditions:** Choose an actual biological unit, measured internal resources, update mechanisms and outcome-linked action constraints.

**Failure or limit:** The apparent control loop relies on unmodelled energy, free maintenance or a resource that has already been exhausted.

<a id="e13"></a>
## E13 · Can these states be told apart?

**Status:** Identifiability gate.

If two hypotheses induce the same law for every accessible history, no policy based only on those histories can distinguish them. A symmetry preserving preparation, transitions and emissions supplies one proof. A new observation must break a target-relevant equivalence, not merely add another reading of the same quantity.

**Required conditions:** State the accessible observation/action family, priors and target. Distinct finite-model signatures do not prove identifiability of all possible mechanisms.

**Failure or limit:** The proposed distinguishing signal is invariant under the same symmetry or requires access to the true hidden label.

**Sources:** [The Observer and the World: Predictive state, lawful forgetting, and the geometry of empirical reality](https://boundedness-atlas.madmanmuzza.chatgpt.site/papers/current-07/) — Retained manuscript abstract, PDF page 1; closing PDF pages 21, 22; [From Predictive State to Viable Action: Action Sufficiency, Safe Diagnosis, and the Operational Recoverability Bound](https://boundedness-atlas.madmanmuzza.chatgpt.site/papers/current-11/) — Retained manuscript abstract, PDF page 1; closing PDF pages 10, 11.

## E14 · Does the distinction change a future?

**Status:** Predictive-sufficiency gate.

Retain a distinction when discarding it changes an admitted future law beyond the declared tolerance. A history can help one forecast and fail to improve another; a bounded signal can still omit essential state.

**Required conditions:** Declare the test family, future horizon and loss; compare with simpler representations at matched acquisition cost.

**Failure or limit:** Extra state does not improve the target forecast, or a compressed state fails an admitted future test.

<a id="e15"></a>
## E15 · Can any available action help enough?

**Status:** Conditional decision ceiling; elementary derivation.

With hidden mechanism H, complete Markov state X, and fixed terminal actions a, let U(H,X,a) be success probability. A non-anticipating nondestructive diagnostic followed by one of those actions has value at most E[max_a U(H,X,a)]. For each realized H,X, even a randomized choice is a convex combination no larger than the maximum; averaging proves the bound. Subtract a strong baseline value to find the improvement headroom.

**Required conditions:** Same prior, fixed pre-decision physical law, complete Markov state, no information about future innovations, same terminal actions and no later feedback. Changing objective, dynamics or actions changes the ceiling.

**Failure or limit:** An improvement target exceeds the available headroom. This rejects the experiment's target within its rules, not all possible biological recovery.

**Sources:** [From Predictive State to Viable Action: Action Sufficiency, Safe Diagnosis, and the Operational Recoverability Bound](https://boundedness-atlas.madmanmuzza.chatgpt.site/papers/current-11/) — Retained manuscript abstract, PDF page 1; closing PDF pages 10, 11.
